About Us

Founded in 2009 with a mission to reduce the ever-increasing electricity bills of MSMEs and to contribute towards environmental protection. We initially started with Energy Audits and gradually expanded our scope to include on-site implementation of energy-saving solutions.

Our unique working model, DSTIS – Design, Sale, Transport, Install, and after-sale Services – ensures a comprehensive and hassle-free experience for our clients.
